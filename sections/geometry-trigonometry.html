Geometry & Trigonometry · GT.5 Coordinate geometry

OFFICIAL LEARNING OUTCOME

POINTS DISTANCE MIDPOINT SLOPE LINES INTERSECTION
Geometry & Trigonometry · GT.5a Points & lines

Find and interpret distance, midpoint, slope, point of intersection, and slopes of parallel and perpendicular lines.

Distance \[ d= \sqrt{ (x_2-x_1)^2+ (y_2-y_1)^2 } \]
Midpoint \[ M= \left( \frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2} \right) \]
Slope \[ m= \frac{y_2-y_1} {x_2-x_1} \]
Geometry & Trigonometry · GT.5a Parallel & perpendicular
Parallel lines

Same slope.

\[ m_1=m_2 \]
Perpendicular lines

Their slopes are negative reciprocals where both slopes are defined.

\[ m_1m_2=-1 \]
Geometry & Trigonometry · GT.5b Graphs in context

Draw graphs of line segments and interpret such graphs in context, including discussing the rate of change and the \(y\)-intercept.

Slope rate of change
\(y\)-intercept starting value when \(x=0\)
\[ y=3x+10 \] starts at 10 and increases by 3 for each unit of \(x\).
Geometry & Trigonometry · GT.5c Equation of a line

Find and interpret the equation of a line in the forms:

\[ y=mx+c \]
\[ y-y_1=m(x-x_1) \]
\[ ax+by+c=0 \]

This includes finding the slope, the \(y\)-intercept, and other points on the line.
