Unifying Strand · Overview · p.10 How students think mathematically

This strand permeates all of the contextual strands and is composed of the six elements of the specification.

There is no specific content linked to this strand; rather, its learning outcomes underpin the rest of the specification. Each learning outcome in this strand is applicable to all of the activities and content of the other four strands.

Unifying Strand · Overview · p.10 Six interdependent elements
  • Building blocks — understand and recall concepts and carry out procedures accurately, effectively, and appropriately.
  • Representation — represent a mathematical situation in different ways and translate flexibly between them.
  • Connections — make connections within and between strands and between mathematics and the real world.
  • Problem solving — investigate patterns, formulate conjectures and engage in tasks where the solution is not immediately obvious.
  • Generalisation and proof — move from specific instances to general mathematical statements and evaluate arguments and proofs.
  • Communication — communicate mathematics effectively in verbal and written form.

Recognise that equality is a relationship in which two mathematical expressions have the same value.

Unifying · Representation · p.14 U.4 Representation

Represent a mathematical situation in a variety of different ways, including numerically, algebraically, graphically, physically, and in words; and interpret, analyse, and compare such representations.

Unifying · Connections · p.14 U.5 Connections

Make connections within and between strands.

Unifying · Connections · p.14 U.6 Connections

Unifying · Problem solving · p.14 U.7 Problem solving

Make sense of a given problem, and if necessary mathematise a situation.

Unifying · Problem solving · p.14 U.8 Problem solving

Apply their knowledge and skills to solve a problem, including decomposing it into manageable parts and/or simplifying it using appropriate assumptions.

Unifying · Problem solving · p.14 U.9 Problem solving

Interpret their solution to a problem in terms of the original question.

Unifying · Problem solving · p.14 U.10 Problem solving

Evaluate different possible solutions to a problem, including evaluating the reasonableness of the solutions, and exploring possible improvements and/or limitations of the solutions, if any.

Unifying · Generalisation and proof · p.14 U.11 Generalisation and proof

Generate general mathematical statements or conjectures based on specific instances.

Unifying · Generalisation and proof · p.14 U.12 Generalisation and proof

Generate and evaluate mathematical arguments and proofs.

Communicate mathematics effectively: justify their reasoning, interpret their results, explain their conclusions, and use the language and notation of mathematics to express mathematical ideas precisely.

THE UNIFYING STRAND CONTINUES

Each learning outcome in this strand is applicable to all of the activities and content of the other four strands.

The other four are the contextual strands: Number, Geometry & Trigonometry, Algebra & Functions, and Statistics & Probability.

As you work through them, some Unifying outcomes may be very obvious. Others simply become part of doing mathematics naturally and well.
