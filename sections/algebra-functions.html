Algebra & Functions · AF.3 Equivalent expressions

OFFICIAL LEARNING OUTCOME

Apply the properties of arithmetic operations and factorisation to generate equivalent expressions and develop and use appropriate strategies.

Equivalent means different appearance — same value.
\[ 3(x+4)=3x+12 \]
Algebra & Functions · AF.3a Add, subtract & simplify

Students develop strategies to add, subtract and simplify:

  • linear expressions in one or more variables with coefficients in \(\mathbb Q\);
  • quadratic expressions in one variable with coefficients in \(\mathbb Z\);
  • expressions of the form \[ \frac{a}{bx+c}, \qquad a,b,c\in\mathbb Z. \]
Algebra & Functions · AF.3b Multiply expressions

Multiply expressions of the forms:

\[ a(bx+cy+d) \] \[ a(bx^2+cx+d) \] \[ ax(bx^2+cx+d) \]

where \(a,b,c,d\in\mathbb Z\).

Structure each term inside the bracket is multiplied.
Algebra & Functions · AF.3b Multiply binomials

\[ (ax+b)(cx+d) \]

and

\[ (ax+b)(cx^2+dx+e) \]

where \(a,b,c,d,e\in\mathbb Z\).

\(acx^2\)
\(adx\)

Algebra & Functions · AF.3c Algebraic division

Divide quadratic and cubic expressions by linear expressions, where all coefficients are integers and there is no remainder.

\[ \frac{x^2+5x+6}{x+2}=x+3 \]

A useful check is to multiply the answer by the divisor and recover the original expression.

Algebra & Functions · AF.3d Factorising quadratics

Students work flexibly between expanded and factorised forms including:

\[ dx^2+bx \] \[ x^2+bx+c \] \[ ax^2+bx+c \]

where the coefficients satisfy the conditions specified in the learning outcome.

\[ x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3) \]
Algebra & Functions · AF.3d Difference of squares
\[ x^2-a^2=(x-a)(x+a) \]

and more generally:

\[ a^2x^2-b^2y^2 = (ax-by)(ax+by) \]
Notice square − square → conjugate factors.
