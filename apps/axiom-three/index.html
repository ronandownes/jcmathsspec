What you discovered

The number of degrees in an angle is between 0° and 360°. An ordinary angle has fewer than 180°.

A straight angle has 180°.

Given ray [AB and a number d between 0 and 180, there is exactly one ray from A on each side of line AB that makes an ordinary angle of d degrees with [AB.

If D is inside ∠BAC, then |∠BAC| = |∠BAD| + |∠DAC|.
