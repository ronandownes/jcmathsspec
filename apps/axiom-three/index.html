Discovery: the greatest angle measure is 360°, one complete turn. Continue around and the measure begins again at 1°, 2°, 3° … rather than 361°, 362°, 363°.
Two senses: the same pair of rays can be approached clockwise or anticlockwise. The two measures complete one full turn.
Convention: unless a reflex angle is explicitly requested, we use the ordinary angle between the rays. Its measure is less than 180°. A straight angle is 180°.
