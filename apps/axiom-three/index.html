What you discovered

The degree-measure of an angle lies between 0° and 360°. An ordinary angle is less than 180°, and a straight angle has measure 180°.

For a chosen measure between 0° and 180°, there is exactly one ray on each side of line AB that makes that ordinary angle with ray AB.

If D lies inside ∠BAC, then |∠BAC| = |∠BAD| + |∠DAC|.
