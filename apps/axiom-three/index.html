Axiom 3 · Protractor Axiom

The degree-measure of an angle is between 0° and 360°. The degree-measure of an ordinary angle is less than 180°.

A straight angle has 180°.

Given ray [AB and a number d between 0 and 180, there is exactly one ray from A on each side of line AB making an ordinary angle of d degrees with [AB.

If D is inside ∠BAC, then |∠BAC| = |∠BAD| + |∠DAC|.

Angle types

0°: null   0°–180°: ordinary   180°: straight   180°–360°: reflex   360°: full.
