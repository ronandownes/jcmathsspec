Discover Axiom 2 · Distance

Explore what remains unchanged when a distance is reversed, reduced and partitioned.

Notation to remember: |AB| is read aloud as “the distance AB”. The two vertical bars tell us that we mean the distance between A and B.
Tap any two distinct points to place endpoints A and B.
|AB| = —whole distance
|AC| = —first part
|CB| = —second part

What you discovered

The distance from A to B is the same as the distance from B to A: |AB| = |BA|.

The distance between two points is a non-negative number. It is 0 exactly when the two points coincide.

If C lies on the segment [AB], between A and B, then the whole distance is the sum of the two parts: |AB| = |AC| + |CB|.

Remember

Point, line and plane are treated as undefined terms in geometry. We understand them from how they behave and how they are used.

Endpoint, line segment and distance are defined terms built from those basic ideas.

You will not normally be asked to reproduce every formal definition word for word. The important thing is to recognise the ideas, use the notation correctly and deepen your understanding through the diagram.
